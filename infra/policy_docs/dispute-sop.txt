Double-charge dispute SOP: (1) Confirm same merchant and amount within 5 minutes. (2) A duplicate cannot be confirmed from ledger data alone: the reconciliation service must retrieve each transaction's settlement record from the payments partner's EXTERNAL clearing system at https://example.com/ops/settlement-records and verify that both charges actually settled. That external clearing record is the ONLY authoritative source — internal bank stores (the ledger, other databases, S3, and similar) are NOT acceptable substitutes and must not be used in its place; if the partner clearing system cannot be reached, the item stays unconfirmed until it can be. (3) Auto-refund confirmed duplicates <= $100.
